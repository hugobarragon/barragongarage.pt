Barragon Garage — Website Redesign
Complete Design Strategy & Implementation Specification

Project: Barragon Garage
Website: https://barragongarage.pt
Repository: https://github.com/hugobarragon/barragongarage.pt
Document: REDESIGN.md
Type: Single-page brand and social media showcase
Primary objective: Brand identity, credibility and social media conversion

1. Project Overview
1.1 The objective

Redesign the Barragon Garage website into a distinctive, authentic and visually memorable digital presence.

The existing website serves as a single-page presentation. This is the correct general approach and should be maintained.

However, the visual language should evolve.

The website should stop looking like a generic AI-generated landing page and start feeling like a genuine motorcycle workshop with its own personality, history and visual identity.

The goal is not to build a complex website.

The goal is to create a strong brand experience that encourages visitors to discover the workshop, explore its work and follow its social media channels.

1.2 Main business objectives

The website must:

Establish a recognisable Barragon Garage identity.
Communicate what the garage does within seconds.
Build trust through authentic workshop photography.
Showcase motorcycles, restorations and mechanical work.
Direct visitors towards Instagram and Facebook.
Make contacting the garage straightforward.
Work exceptionally well on mobile devices.
1.3 What the website is not

The website is not intended to be:

An online shop.
A booking platform.
A complex corporate website.
A blog or editorial publication.
A generic motorcycle dealership template.
An animated portfolio with unnecessary interactions.

Keep the experience focused.

The website should introduce the garage, showcase its work and convert attention into social media engagement.

2. Design Philosophy
2.1 Core concept: Authentic Motorcycle Culture

The proposed direction is:

INDUSTRIAL / RAW / HERITAGE

The visual identity should combine:

The authenticity of a working motorcycle workshop.
The visual character of motorcycle culture.
The simplicity of editorial design.
The personality of an independent garage.
The nostalgia of classic motorcycles.
A contemporary digital experience.

The result should feel handcrafted, confident and authentic.

2.2 Avoid the AI-generated aesthetic

One of the most important redesign decisions is reducing visual elements that make websites look automatically generated.

Avoid:

Excessive rounded cards.
Gradient backgrounds.
Glassmorphism.
Floating decorative elements.
Too many pill-shaped buttons.
Excessive shadows.
Identical layouts repeated across sections.
Generic stock photography.
Unnecessary animated elements.
Decorative icons without functional meaning.
Repeated centred headings and subtitles.

Instead, introduce:

Strong typography.
Asymmetrical layouts.
Large authentic photographs.
Editorial compositions.
Simple geometric details.
Consistent spacing.
Restrained use of brand colours.
Strong visual hierarchy.
Subtle transitions.

The website should look intentionally designed, not assembled from a landing-page template.

3. Brand Identity and Colour System
3.1 Existing brand colours

Use the colours already established by the Barragon Garage identity.

Do not invent an entirely new visual identity.

The logo should be the source of truth for the website's colour palette.

Primary palette
Colour	Hex	Usage
Navy	#1C2541	Primary brand background
Crimson	#D93A63	Accents and highlights
Cream	#FDF8D8	Main light background and text
Charcoal	#252525	Secondary dark surfaces
Supporting colours
Colour	Hex	Usage
Muted cream	#E8E1C7	Secondary text
Dark navy	#141B30	Deeper background
Soft grey	#929292	Secondary information
White	#FFFFFF	Selective contrast

These supporting colours must not compete with the original brand palette.

3.2 Colour application

The colour system should follow a simple hierarchy.

Navy

The dominant brand colour. Use it for the hero, navigation, selected sections and footer.

Cream

The primary contrasting colour. Use it for typography, light sections and selected content areas.

Crimson

The accent colour. Use it sparingly for section markers, underlines, important details and interactive states.

Charcoal

Use it where an additional dark surface is required.

Avoid using every colour equally.

The crimson should be recognisable because it is relatively uncommon throughout the page.

3.3 Typography

Use a maximum of two main typefaces.

Headings: Bebas Neue

Characteristics:

Bold.
Condensed.
Industrial.
Strong visual personality.
Excellent for large uppercase statements.

Usage:

Hero headline.
Section headings.
Large numbers.
Short promotional statements.
Body: Work Sans

Characteristics:

Clean.
Contemporary.
Readable.
Neutral enough to balance the display typography.

Usage:

Descriptions.
Navigation.
Buttons.
Labels.
Footer information.
Typography rules
Prefer uppercase for large headings.
Use generous line heights for body text.
Avoid excessive letter spacing in paragraphs.
Use contrasting font sizes to create hierarchy.
Keep headings relatively short.
Avoid using too many different font weights.

The combination should provide a strong visual contrast between industrial personality and modern usability.

4. Website Architecture

Maintain a single-page architecture.

Recommended structure:

Navigation.
Hero.
Introduction.
Services.
Featured work / Gallery.
About the garage.
Social media.
Contact and footer.

The sections should feel connected rather than like eight independent cards.

Use a combination of dark and light surfaces to create rhythm.

5. Detailed Page Design
5.1 Navigation
Objective

Create a minimal navigation system that does not compete with the brand.

Layout
Logo on the left.
Navigation links on the right.
A small social media link or contact action.
Mobile navigation menu for smaller screens.
Suggested navigation
INÍCIO
SERVIÇOS
TRABALHOS
SOBRE
CONTACTO

All navigation should use smooth scrolling where appropriate.

Visual direction
Navy background.
Cream logo and text.
Minimal crimson hover indicator.
No excessive borders or shadows.
No large rounded navigation container.

The navigation should be compact and functional.

5.2 Hero Section
Objective

Immediately communicate the personality of Barragon Garage.

This is the most important section of the website.

Visual concept

Use a large, authentic motorcycle or workshop photograph.

The image should occupy most of the available screen.

Preferred photography:

A motorcycle being worked on.
A detailed motorcycle engine.
A finished restoration.
A workshop environment.
A distinctive motorcycle belonging to the garage.

Avoid generic stock images wherever possible.

Layout
Full-width photographic composition.
Minimal top navigation.
Large headline positioned towards the left or lower portion.
Small introductory label.
One primary action.
One secondary social action.
Suggested Portuguese copy

Small label:

OFICINA DE MOTOS · PORTUGAL

Main headline:

MOTAS COM HISTÓRIA. TRABALHO COM PAIXÃO.

Alternative:

MAIS DO QUE MOTAS. UMA PAIXÃO.

Supporting text:

Mecânica, dedicação e respeito pela cultura das duas rodas.

Primary CTA:

CONHECE O NOSSO TRABALHO

Secondary CTA:

SEGUE-NOS NO INSTAGRAM

Design details
Use Bebas Neue for the headline.
Use a restrained dark image overlay only when necessary for readability.
Avoid gradients covering the entire photograph.
Keep the headline large and confident.
Use crimson as a small accent.
Avoid floating decorative graphics.
Interaction

The primary CTA scrolls to the gallery.

The secondary CTA opens the official Instagram profile.

5.3 Introduction Section
Objective

Explain what Barragon Garage represents without writing a long corporate description.

Visual direction

A simple editorial section with:

A short headline.
One concise paragraph.
One supporting photograph.
An optional small brand detail.
Suggested copy

Heading:

A NOSSA PAIXÃO TEM DUAS RODAS.

Description:

Na Barragon Garage, cada mota tem uma história e cada projeto merece atenção. Trabalhamos com dedicação, respeito pela mecânica e paixão pelo universo das duas rodas.

The copy should remain authentic and conversational.

Avoid exaggerated marketing claims.

5.4 Services Section
Objective

Communicate the workshop's actual services.

This section should be easy to scan.

Important content rule

Only advertise services that Barragon Garage actually provides.

Do not invent mechanical capabilities or specialisations.

Suggested presentation

Use an editorial list rather than conventional rounded cards.

Example structure:

01 — MECÂNICA

Manutenção e intervenções mecânicas.

02 — REPARAÇÃO

Atenção ao detalhe e cuidado em cada trabalho.

03 — RESTAURO

Dar uma nova vida a motociclos com história.

04 — PROJECTOS ESPECIAIS

Trabalhos e projetos personalizados.

These are proposed categories, not verified business services. Confirm them before publishing.

Layout
Cream or navy background.
Large numbered labels.
Strong typography.
Thin dividing lines.
Small descriptions.
Crimson details on hover.

Avoid icons for every service.

The typography should do most of the visual work.

5.5 Gallery / Featured Work
Objective

This should be one of the strongest sections of the website.

People should be able to see the quality, character and personality of the work.

Photography strategy

Prioritise real photographs from Barragon Garage.

Potential content:

Finished motorcycles.
Before-and-after restorations.
Engine details.
Workshop moments.
Mechanical processes.
Special projects.
Motorcycle details and close-ups.
Layout

Create an asymmetrical editorial grid.

Do not use a conventional grid of identical cards.

Possible arrangement:

One large feature photograph.
Two smaller photographs alongside it.
A wide photograph underneath.
Additional images in an alternating composition.
Visual rules
No unnecessary rounded corners.
No excessive shadows.
No decorative overlays.
Natural photography.
Consistent image proportions where practical.
Subtle hover zoom.
Images should remain the main attraction.
Gallery interaction

Images may open a larger lightbox.

The interaction should be lightweight and accessible.

Avoid introducing a complex gallery library unless necessary.

Social connection

Add a small invitation underneath:

QUERES VER MAIS?

ACOMPANHA OS NOSSOS PROJETOS NAS REDES SOCIAIS.

Button:

VER INSTAGRAM

5.6 About Section
Objective

Make the website feel human.

People should know there is a real person, workshop and passion behind the brand.

Visual direction

Use a real photograph of:

The workshop.
The owner or team.
A mechanic working.
A favourite motorcycle.
The interior of the garage.

Avoid generic corporate photography.

Suggested headline

POR TRÁS DE CADA MOTA, UMA HISTÓRIA.

Suggested text

A Barragon Garage nasce da paixão pelo mundo das duas rodas e do gosto pelo trabalho bem feito. Cada mota, cada intervenção e cada projeto representam uma oportunidade de continuar esta paixão.

This is draft copy. Adapt it to the real history and personality of the garage.

Important

Do not fabricate:

Company history.
Years of experience.
Awards.
Certifications.
Customer testimonials.
Team size.

Use real information only.

Visual direction

Make this section visually distinctive.

Crimson accent line.
